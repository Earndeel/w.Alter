# Elie UGC iteration authority

For the Elie UGC production, Monsieur explicitly prioritizes the best result and authorizes generation iterations without a fixed count limit. Do not repeatedly request permission for targeted retries within that approved creative scope. Preserve cost receipts, diagnose each failure, compare candidates, and move to postproduction once quality gates pass rather than looping indefinitely.

This is project-specific authorization, not blanket permission for other projects, credit purchases, provider/model substitutions, or publication.

Separate mandatory brief failures (wrong selfie camera POV, missing commercial benefit, unwanted speech, distracting morphology) from assistant-chosen editorial preferences (a precise pointing gesture or reframing). Prefer clean local repairs when sufficient; regenerate for material improvements. Do not turn an invented editorial requirement into a user-facing blocker.
