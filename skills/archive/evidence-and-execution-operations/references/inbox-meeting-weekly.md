# Inbox, meeting, and weekly-review branches

## Inbox triage

Resolve account, folders or labels, time window, unread/all scope, thread bound, and permitted actions before retrieval. Default to read plus draft; "handle my inbox" does not authorize send, delete, or archive.

Retrieve complete relevant threads with structured filters and pagination. Classify each as urgent reply, reply, action without reply, waiting, reference, or noise, and record the sender request, deadline, prior commitments, attachments, missing facts, and reason for the disposition.

Draft in full thread context, answer material questions, preserve tone, and avoid invented commitments or attachment/link claims. Present recipient/thread, action, draft summary, deadline, and risk for approval. After an approved mutation, read back provider state; inspect Sent before retrying an ambiguous send.

## Meeting follow-through

Establish title, date, participants, source files, transcript completeness, and speaker/time evidence. Separate decisions, proposals, commitments, questions, blockers, risks, dependencies, and context; do not promote brainstorming to a decision.

Normalize every commitment to an outcome, explicit owner or `unresolved`, explicit due date or `unresolved`, dependency, observable acceptance condition, and source reference. Search the owning tracker before creating anything, preserve owner/date/status conflicts for confirmation, and distinguish proposed creates from updates.

Draft minutes, tickets, and follow-up messages without publishing them. Apply only approved writes, attach provenance, and search for the provenance marker before retrying a timeout.

## Weekly review

Freeze timezone, review period, planning horizon, authoritative task/project store, calendars, inboxes, notes, and permitted writes. Cover the completed week and the next one to two weeks; reconcile commitments, deadlines, capacity, capture inboxes, active projects, waiting items, stale records, duplicate records, and contradictions.

Give each active project an outcome, next action, owner, deadline, blocker, last meaningful activity, and source. Keep missing values visible, mark paused or deferred work explicitly, and do not treat silence as completion. Choose a small capacity-aware set of weekly outcomes without filling every free hour, then apply only approved updates and read each changed record back.
