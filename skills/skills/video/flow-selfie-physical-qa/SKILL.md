# Selfie physical POV and retry selection

- State that the camera IS the speaker-held device outside the image, with foreshortened forearm exiting a frame corner and recording hand/device entirely outside frame; ban visible whole phone, phone back, camera module, mirror and third-party operator. Avoid ambiguous `phone edge` instructions: generators can depict a phone-holder filmed by another camera.
- Reject a visible complete phone back facing the viewer as proof of that phone's front-camera capture. Native-video model audits may falsely equate holding a phone with self-filming; timestamped pixels and physical geometry override blanket compliance conclusions.
- Distinguish the filming arm from an unnecessary free-hand prop. Inspect native video plus dense timestamped frames around prop entry/exit; remove an unnecessary prop in the next authorized prompt rather than forcing a paper-folder gesture that morphs into a phone.
- Once physical POV succeeds, preserve that source as fallback. For a materially missing panel gesture, condition a new authorized variant on an inspected still from the successful fictional selfie; place the required action early, give its actual spatial target, and compare before selecting.
