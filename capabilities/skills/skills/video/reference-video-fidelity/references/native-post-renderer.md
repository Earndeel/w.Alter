# Native-audio local postproduction

- Keep local renderer work and non-deliverable previews separate from shared generation prompts/manifests; another worker can be changing the generation inventory concurrently, so a changed shared hash is not proof the renderer edited it.
- Preserve each visible speaker's image and original audio in the same edit interval. Restrict a separate native-audio J-cut to shots without a visible speaking face, then require exact source-time continuation into that speaker's selfie.
- Use lossless PCM audio between cuts and perform only one final AAC encode. For speed-one continuity tests, compare assembled decoded PCM byte-for-byte with the expected real native-source intervals; a stream-presence check cannot prove native continuity.
- Distinguish AAC content duration from decoder packet padding. Count video frames, check the declared audio/container duration and disclose up to one final AAC packet of raw decoded padding instead of reporting it as timeline drift or fabricating an exact decoded sample count.
- Reject overlapping, zero-length or malformed ASR word timestamps without silently rewriting them. Preserve the raw sidecar and ask the supervising editor to validate corrections; actual ASR output is not automatically a verified transcript.
- Attach standalone punctuation to the preceding lexical word in a separately reviewed sidecar, but do not inherit an ASR punctuation token's silent interval as spoken-word duration; record the original indices, discarded interval and editorial reason against the immutable raw hash.
- Preserve the source audio rate in provenance when decoding 32 kHz native AAC into canonical 48 kHz PCM for assembly; compare the decoded/resampled source intervals byte-for-byte and describe this as speed-one continuity, not AAC stream-copy or bitstream identity.
- For a design-only preview with unresolved ASR errors, keep the native audio whole and use an explicitly marked unchanged, non-overlapping word subset. Do not invent ad words, replace them with TTS or label the partial specimen a validated transcription.
- Store word times in native-source coordinates and remap them through trims and any explicitly allowed common audiovisual retiming. Merge a native word spanning contiguous camera splits or J-cut boundaries so it receives one active-word highlight rather than duplicate caption tokens.
- Require exact frame-aligned edit duration instead of padding the master with frozen people or an external audio bed. Make voice retiming an explicit opt-in; use camera splits and constant digital punch-ins for visual pace.
- Split an overlong caption group into smaller groups of the same timestamped tokens when measured glyph widths cannot fit two lines at a readable font size; do not silently shrink to illegibility or change the transcript to satisfy a nominal word count.
- Do not symmetrically pad adjacent caption groups when the last word of one touches the first word of the next: independent preroll/postroll creates simultaneous messages. Use exact lexical group bounds or jointly solve display intervals while protecting every actual word, and regression-test touching groups before rendering the real master.
- Check VP9/Opus playback with the actual ended event and decoded video-frame count; permit at most one Opus packet of container padding while keeping the canonical MP4 and video-frame timeline exact. Preserve the unsupported-codec MP4 test separately instead of relabeling it as passed.
- Inspect the installed FFmpeg filter ranges before normalizing a quiet native music bed: low integrated loudness does not require an out-of-range true-peak target. Validate the final mix's measured loudness and peak, not only the requested normalization settings.
- Diagnose decoded native PCM and final AAC peaks separately before blaming music gain: AAC may overshoot full scale even when the canonical PCM peaks at unity. Preserve native premix PCM provenance and apply explicitly reported linear master attenuation to the final mix when needed; do not synthesize or retime speech to solve headroom.
- Recover an audio-only mastering failure from the retained lossless native voice and original instrumental bed while stream-copying the already validated video. Never reencode the failed mixed AAC as the new master; verify video bitstream identity, independent native premix PCM continuity, actual decoded final peak/true peak, frame count and EOF alignment.
- Treat a sparse chronological contact sheet as sampling evidence, not a complete transcript: short caption groups may fall between frames. Before changing grammar or declaring an omitted phrase, cross-check the unprimed complete audio transcript and extract an actual frame inside the disputed caption interval.
- Measure the actual isolated post-duck music stem against the voice during voiced windows; nominal gain settings do not establish a usable balance, because a quiet generated bed plus attenuation and sidechain compression can effectively erase the music. Decode the delivered master as well and distinguish music energy from AAC residual error; do not change the balance without an explicit creative decision.
- Inspect sample discontinuities at every edited native-audio boundary, even when the paired audiovisual cut is frame-aligned and ASR words appear intact. Compare an edit's step with the original contiguous source waveform; byte-perfect assembly provenance does not prove a pop-free or phoneme-safe join. Choose a safe paired cut or an explicitly allowed audio transition, preserving lip sync and without silently removing words.
- For an audio-only correction with picture/timeline locked, regression-test a demonstrated edit spike on the real native PCM before repair. A short sample-count-preserving cosine ramp on each side of only the defective join can remove its step without moving speech; verify untouched samples outside the ramp, final decoded AAC steps at every cut, complete unprimed ASR and identical video elementary-stream hash. Do not blanket-fade safe joins or infer preserved phonemes from a zero boundary sample alone.
- Level each actual edited scene from correctly trimmed loudness measurements before calibrating the retained instrumental stem against the final voice in voiced windows. Use an explicit measured target and verify the encoded master; do not stack fixed attenuation with aggressive sidechain ducking by habit, or add EQ/denoising/compression without evidence that those processes address the complaint.
- Place interval trimming before the loudness meter in the FFmpeg filtergraph (atrim, asetpts, then loudnorm/ebur128); output-side -ss/-t can leave the meter analyzing the whole input and falsely label whole-file readings as scene measurements.
- Reject an automated audio review as a listening gate when its per-file timestamps exceed actual EOF, it confuses multiple audio inputs into one timeline, or its masking claims contradict isolated-stem measurements. Preserve the rejected observation as evidence, disclose uncertainty, and never use a fluent model critique or passing peak checks as creative audio approval.
