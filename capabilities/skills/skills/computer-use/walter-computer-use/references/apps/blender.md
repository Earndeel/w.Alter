# Blender

Blender mixes standard native surfaces with custom canvas/viewport regions. Menus and dialogs may be AX-addressable; viewport controls and rendered canvases may not expose reliable AX targets.

When repeated AX evidence does not expose the required viewport affordance, prefer one targeted visual observation over repeated full-tree queries. Preserve exact window-local geometry and fresh visual refs. Recovery dialogs and startup surprises are returned to Hermes; never choose Recover/Skip internally.
