# Dating Response Style — Message Crafting Guidelines

## Core Principles

1. **Language of the conversation** — if she writes in English, respond in English (not always English)
2. **3 propositions systématiques** — always offer 3 response options
3. **Sexy, drôle, provoquant, mais pertinent** — reference her profile/context
4. **No direct physical meetup pressure** — teasing, not "let's meet up"
5. **Max 2 courtes parties** — short, punchy, not paragraphs
6. **No repetition between options** — different structures, different keywords, different angles
7. **Timestamps: always convert to UTC+7 (Bangkok)** — never use raw UTC

## Emoji Usage

- Vary — not every message needs an emoji
- 😏 — playful/teasing (default for flirty contexts)
- 😊 — warm/genuine (for serious/casual contexts)
- 😈 — bold/suggestive (when she's already provocative)
- 😄 — lighthearted (for casual/boring openers)
- Sometimes no emoji at all — mix it up

## Response Archetypes

### 1. Very Flirty/Provocative (she's sexually forward)
**Pattern**: Bold + teasing, acknowledge the invite without being desperate
- "Careful, I might take you up on that before you even finish your drink 😏"
- "Dangerous question from a girl who's supposed to be at a party 😏 I'm more of a 'show don't tell' type"
- "Say less. When's the party over? 😏"

### 2. Casual/Curious (she's friendly, asking basic questions)
**Pattern**: Warm + playful, flip the question back with personality
- "Better now that you asked 😏 What about you — what's keeping [name] busy tonight?"
- "Still surviving Bangkok 😄 What about you — got any fun plans or just vibing?"

### 3. Serious/Bio-focused (she wants something meaningful)
**Pattern**: Genuine + curious, reference specific profile details
- "Heyy 😊 A scrub nurse who spends her off-days on boats? Which one's the real you?"
- "Heyy 😊 So what does a kindhearted nurse do to unwind in Bangkok?"

### 4. Low Energy ("How are you", "Hey", "Hii")
**Pattern**: Relance with open-ended question, inject personality
- "Still alive in Bangkok 😄 What about you — got any fun plans tonight?"
- "Good! You seem like trouble in the best way. What are you up to? 😏"

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ "Hey! Would you like to grab a drink this week?" (too transactional)
- ❌ "You're beautiful" (generic, no effort)
- ❌ Long paragraphs (she won't read them)
- ❌ Immediately asking to meet (pressuring)
- ❌ Copy-paste vibes (each response should feel tailored)
- ❌ Over-using emojis (1 max per message)

## Conversation Priority Matrix

| Signal | Priority | Reasoning |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| "Your move" + flirty energy | 🔴 HIGH | She's engaged and waiting |
| "Your move" + casual | 🟡 MEDIUM | Worth responding but lower conversion |
| "Your move" + boring/low effort | 🟠 LOW-MED | Relance but manage expectations |
| No "Your move" + interesting bio | 🟢 OPPORTUNISTIC | You messaged last, but could double-text if strong profile |
| Expired conversation | ⚫ SKIP | Can't recover |

## Bumble-Specific Context (Bangkok)

- Many Thai women are direct/blunt in English — this is cultural, not aggressive
- "No drama" in bio = she's had bad experiences, wants chill vibes
- Party/nightlife photos are common and don't mean "easy" — cultural norm
- IG handles in bio = she wants followers, not necessarily a date
- Scrub nurse / medical professional = common in Bangkok, high-status job
- "Not looking for hook up" = she means it, don't try to sexualize
- Frequent drinking = normal social activity in Bangkok, not a red flag
